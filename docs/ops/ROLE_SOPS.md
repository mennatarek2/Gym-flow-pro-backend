# Gym role SOPs — Final Role Assignment (ops)

Assignable operational tasks. Engineering does not “close” these — gym staff execute them.

## Manager

### M-01 — Running Costs posting SOP (P0)

1. Open shift before any **cash** running cost.
2. Post OpEx only via Running Costs catalog (Utilities / Operations / Rent & Property / …).
3. Never post payroll or supplier AP as OpEx.
4. Spot-check Owner **Cost to run**: OpEx line excludes payroll.

### M-02 — Partial pay / collect training (P0)

1. Train desk: underpay ≠ discount.
2. Add Member: amount &lt; plan price → set due date → creates AmountDue.
3. Collect later on Member 360 → Collect Payment (one sale at a time).

### M-03 — Payroll period COMPLETE (P1)

1. Close/approve payroll periods overlapping the Owner month.
2. Confirm Owner Net profit can display (`payrollCoverageStatus = COMPLETE`).

### M-04 — Class schedule / capacity (P1)

1. Ensure sessions exist for upcoming days.
2. Coach + capacity accurate so Member App upcoming classes are not empty/wrong.

## Trainer

### T-01 — Schedule / coach / capacity (P1)

Keep session coach, time, and seats accurate after generation. Member App seats = capacity − booked/checked_in.

## Receptionist

### R-01 — Open shift before cash (P0)

Open shift before cash OpEx or cash membership payment / collect.

### R-02 — Partial payment + due date (P0)

On Add Member underpay: use partial payment + due date. Confirm sale succeeds and Outstanding appears on 360.

### R-03 — Collect outstanding (P0)

Member 360 → Collect Payment → pick sale → pay → Outstanding updates.

### R-04 — Reception class booking (P1)

After member browses classes in the app, book/pay at desk; capacity must decrease.

## Owner

### O-01 — Monthly KPI review (P1)

Review Cost to run / Gross / Net (not Sales Report alone). Use Financial glossary on Owner dashboard. Explain OpEx vs payroll vs AP vs cash.

### D-01 … D-04

Remain **PRODUCT DECISION** — record policy in writing; do not treat as desk data entry.
